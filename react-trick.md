Here are some attractive React tricks you could add to your landing page:

**1. Parallax Scrolling Effect**
- Make the hero image move at different speeds as you scroll
- Creates depth and modern feel
- Works great with your existing Framer Motion setup

**2. Scroll-triggered Counter Animations**
- Animate numbers counting up (e.g., "500+ Projects", "100+ Clients")
- Triggers when section comes into view
- Eye-catching for stats/achievements section

**3. Magnetic Button Effect**
- Buttons that slightly follow the cursor when hovering
- Adds interactive, premium feel
- Works well on your CTA buttons

**4. Text Reveal on Scroll**
- Text that reveals character-by-character as you scroll down
- Creates storytelling effect
- Great for section headings

**5. 3D Tilt Card Effect**
- Service cards that tilt based on mouse position
- Adds depth and interactivity
- Perfect for your Services section

**6. Gradient Background Animation**
- Slowly moving gradient background
- Subtle but premium feel
- Can be applied to hero or sections

**7. Mouse Trail Effect**
- Subtle particles following cursor
- Adds playful interactivity
- Can be themed to your brand colors

**8. Smooth Page Transitions**
- Fade/slide transitions between sections
- Makes navigation feel fluid
- You already have Framer Motion, so this is easy
